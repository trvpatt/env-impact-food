GROCERY COST + CLIMATE

Could a plant-forward basket cost less?

Vegetarian patterns replacing some animal foods with legumes and whole grains were among the most affordable sustainable patterns in a 150-country model.

22–34% lowerModeled diet cost vs. current diets, on average across upper-middle- to high-income countries.
Climate costs includedThe study added estimated climate damage and diet-related health-care costs to food prices.

A modeled comparison using 2017 prices, not a local grocery quote. Range applies across modeled patterns, not separately to each toggle.

Sources and how to read this

Springmann et al. modeled 463 food items in 150 countries. Vegetarian and vegan patterns were generally among the most affordable, especially when legumes and whole grains replaced animal products.
